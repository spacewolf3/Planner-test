# House Book

A household book for cleaning, upkeep, appliances and the house cleaners' visits. Built for a phone, one page, no install.

## Sections

| Tab | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **To do** | What's due now, this week or this month, narrowed to the time you have (10 minutes to 2 hours). Cleaning and home upkeep are listed separately. Includes a shopping list from "Need supplies" and tasks not set up yet. |
| **House cleaners** | Log each visit and check what they did; those tasks reset as done "by Cleaners." Keeps the usual checklist, cost and house-rules notes to send them. |
| **Cleaning** | Every cleaning task, including bathrooms, wood floors, silk and wool rugs, instruments and plants. |
| **Home** | Appliances (brand, model, serial, warranty, manual link, notes) with their own care tasks, plus upkeep for home systems, safety, pool and pond. |
| **Week** | Tasks done, time, money, savings compared with hiring out, who did what, and the cleaners' visits. |
| **Guide** | Never-mix rules, notes for this house, and the gentle and stronger cleaning kits. |
| **Settings** (gear icon) | House name, who logs tasks, cleaner schedule, cost and notes, savings rate, backup. |

## Where data is kept

Opened as a claude.ai artifact, the data is saved to your claude.ai account and syncs between devices. Opened as a plain file, it saves in that browser only.
