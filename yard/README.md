# Yard & Irrigation Plan

A map of the yard and sprinkler system, with zone schedules, a parts list, seasonal care and a repair log.

## Sections

| Tab | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Map** | Trace over a base image (satellite screenshot, plot plan or sketch). Draw mainline, laterals, drip, valve wire and sleeves. Place heads, valves, backflow, controller, shut-offs, trees and plants, and outline lawns, beds and hardscape. Points snap to heads, valves and other pipes. Measure something of known length to set the scale; pipe lengths and areas then come out in feet. Heads show their spray coverage from radius and arc. Highlight one zone at a time, and attach a photo to anything, such as the inside of a valve box. |
| **Zones** | Station number, run time, days and start time, with heads, GPM, pipe length and gallons per week for each zone. A parts list (head models, nozzles, pipe by size and material) and a plant list. |
| **Care** | Seasonal irrigation and landscape upkeep, such as start-up, blowout, backflow test, filters and battery, with due status. |
| **Log** | Repairs, installs and adjustments with parts and cost, plus completed care tasks. |

## Where data is kept

Opened as a claude.ai artifact, the plan (and any photos) is saved with the artifact on claude.ai. Opened as a plain file, it saves in that browser only. None of it is in this repository.
