# The Zhang-Wolfe House

A house book for one family: a maintenance tracker and log. Routine cleaning isn't tracked; deep-cleaning jobs that fall outside a normal clean are part of the maintenance schedule. The app is a single file, `app/index.html`, published as a claude.ai artifact.

- **House**: overview of what needs attention (urgent fix-its, overdue maintenance, warranties ending), open fix-its, what's coming up, spending this year by category, recent work, the cleaners' schedule (every other Tuesday) and thirsty plants.
- **Fix-its**: repairs and projects with priority (urgent / soon / someday), status (to do / in progress / waiting / fixed), area, DIY or hired, contact, estimate, notes, photos, and linked receipts.
- **Maintenance**: one recurring schedule for deep cleaning and home systems (due now, next 30 days, a time picker, all tasks by area), seasons (pool closed, pond winter mode), appliances with model numbers, warranties, manuals, care tasks and their own service history, house facts and contacts.
- **Log**: everything done to the house, newest first: receipts (photos or PDFs, with Claude reading the photo to fill in vendor, date and amount), work done, notes, finished maintenance, fixed fix-its and cleaners' visits. Yearly and monthly totals by category, search, filters, and the weekly wrap-up.
- **Plants**: plants by room with watering, photos, light and cat safety.
- **Guide** (book icon) and **settings** (gear): cleaning guide, household names, cleaners, quiet mode, backup.

As a claude.ai artifact the data is saved to your account and syncs. Opened as a plain file, it saves in that browser only.

The cover image and icons (`cover.png`, `icon-512.png`, `icon-180.png`) are rendered from `art-source.html`.
